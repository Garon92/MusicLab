## Original sound designs

The other 32 one-shots were synthesized for MUSICLAB. The horse, duck, owl, cricket and jungle calls are musical imitations. The burp, fart, hiccup, sneeze, raspberry and giggle use shaped noise, pitch contours and vowel resonances. The household, outdoor, toy and space sounds use percussion models, filtered noise and tuned oscillators. They ship as local mono 24 kHz PCM alongside the original recordings.
