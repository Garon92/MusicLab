# Pocket Band sound recordings

41 of the 48 one-shots are excerpts of recordings published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) on Freesound. Each was cut to one call or hit, faded, converted to mono 24 kHz PCM, started within a few milliseconds of its first sound so it lands on the beat, and levelled to the same loudness: the loudest 200 ms (or, for sharp hits, the loudest 50 ms less 4 dB), K-weighted as in ITU-R BS.1770, at -16 LUFS, with peaks (between the samples too) under -1 dBFS. Up to 9 dB of the sharpest peaks are limited; the knock, keys, rain, rattle and pig stay 1.5–4 dB quieter rather than be squashed further. The train combines a toy whistle with two wheel strokes.

## Original sound designs

The spinning top and the six space sounds (laser, flying saucer, robot, rocket, star and radar) were synthesized for MUSICLAB with percussion models, filtered noise and tuned oscillators, and levelled like the recordings.

When the audio of any of these files changes, raise `SOUNDS` in `docs/js/kids-world.js`: browsers keep a copy of every sound they have played and replace it only when that number changes.
