# Pocket Band sound recordings

These local one-shots are excerpts of recordings published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The samples were cut, faded, level-matched and converted to mono 24 kHz PCM for the sequencer. The train combines a toy whistle with two wheel strokes.
